## 1. About this website

This website is operated for **Anima Space**, a well-being, psychological consulting, counselling, coaching, career-counselling, healing, and training centre associated with **Prashanthi Simon**. The website provides information about Anima Space’s services for children, adolescents, adults, parents and guardians, students, educators, and organisations. The centre’s public contact details are **animaspace9@gmail.com** and **+91 98664 10936**. Its listed in-clinic address is **Anima Space Child Psychology Center, Plot 104, Road No. 36, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, Telangana 500033, India**.

The information on this website is intended to help visitors understand the services offered and how to make an enquiry or appointment. It is not intended to create a professional relationship merely because a person visits the website, reads an article, sends an enquiry, or uses a WhatsApp link. A professional relationship begins only when Anima Space expressly accepts a client for services and the relevant informed-consent, intake, and service arrangements have been completed.

## 2. Information is not a substitute for individual care

Website pages, blog articles, examples, descriptions of approaches, frequently asked questions, testimonials, and other materials are provided for general educational and informational purposes. They are not a substitute for an individual assessment, psychological counselling, psychotherapy, medical evaluation, psychiatric care, diagnosis, treatment plan, or advice from a suitably qualified professional who has assessed the person concerned.

Every person’s circumstances are different. A description of a service, approach, exercise, or expected session experience does not mean that the same approach is suitable for every person, and it does not guarantee a particular result. Anima Space does not guarantee that any service will achieve a specific academic, emotional, behavioural, career, family, or personal outcome.

## 3. Scope of services

Anima Space may offer psychological counselling, child and adolescent support, parent guidance, coaching, career counselling, healing or well-being support, and training. The nature, duration, suitability, limitations, and fees of a service will be discussed before the service begins. A booking request is not automatically an acceptance of a client, and Anima Space may recommend a different service, referral, or level of care when that is more appropriate.

The website describes online sessions using Google Meet and in-clinic sessions at the Hyderabad centre. Online services depend on the client’s device, internet connection, location, privacy, and the availability and security of third-party technology. Anima Space cannot guarantee uninterrupted connectivity, complete confidentiality of a client’s device or network, or the performance of a third-party platform.

## 4. No emergency or crisis service

**Anima Space is not an emergency-response service, crisis hotline, ambulance service, hospital, or substitute for urgent medical or psychiatric care.** The website, email, phone, WhatsApp, booking form, and online sessions are not monitored continuously and must not be relied on for immediate help.

If someone is in immediate danger, may harm themselves or another person, has taken an overdose, is experiencing a medical emergency, or cannot remain safe, contact **112 in India**, go to the nearest emergency department, or ask a trusted person to stay with them while urgent help is obtained. Do not wait for a reply to a website form, email, WhatsApp message, or appointment request. 

For 24/7 mental-health crisis and suicide prevention helplines in India:
- **Tele-MANAS (Govt. of India):** **14416** or **1800-891-4416** (Toll-free, 24/7, multi-lingual)
- **KIRAN Mental Health Helpline:** **1800-599-0019** (Toll-free, 24/7)
- **Vandrevala Foundation:** **+91 9999 666 555** (Free, 24/7 crisis counselling)

## 5. Confidentiality and its limits

Anima Space aims to protect the confidentiality of information shared in professional services. Confidentiality is not absolute. Information may need to be used or disclosed where permitted or required by applicable law, where necessary to respond to a serious or imminent risk to a person’s safety, where safeguarding or suspected abuse concerns arise, where a court or lawful authority requires disclosure, where professional consultation or referral is necessary and permitted, or where disclosure is otherwise authorised by the client or lawful guardian.

The exact confidentiality arrangements, including arrangements for children and adolescents, will be explained during intake and recorded in the applicable informed-consent or service agreement. Parents and guardians should understand that a child’s privacy and the parent’s or guardian’s involvement may be balanced differently according to the child’s age, maturity, safety, the service being provided, and applicable law.

## 6. Children and adolescents

Anima Space’s website describes services for children and adolescents, including individuals aged 8–18. A parent or lawful guardian should submit information and provide consent for a person under 18 wherever required. Anima Space may request information reasonably needed to verify authority to act for a child and may refuse or pause a booking if the required consent or safeguarding information is not available.

Parents, guardians, and clients should not include detailed clinical histories, identity documents, highly sensitive information, or crisis disclosures in an open website message, ordinary email, or WhatsApp message unless Anima Space specifically requests that information through an appropriate channel.

## 7. Booking, contact, email, and WhatsApp

The website’s contact form asks for a parent or guardian name, phone number, email address, age group or target, primary concern, and an optional message. The booking flow asks for a parent or guardian name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and an optional child age or brief note. These details are used to respond to enquiries, assess the requested service at a preliminary level, administer appointments, and communicate booking information, as described in the **Anima Space Privacy Policy**.

WhatsApp, email, telephone networks, Google Meet, hosting providers, calendar systems, and other third-party services may have their own terms and privacy practices. Anima Space does not control those services. Avoid sending highly sensitive information through a channel that is not specifically designated for secure clinical communication.

The website may link to Google Maps, WhatsApp, Google Meet, social-media pages, external articles, helplines, or other third-party websites. Those services are operated by their respective providers and are governed by their own terms and privacy notices. A link is provided for convenience and does not constitute endorsement, warranty, or a representation that the linked service is suitable for a particular person.

## 9. Testimonials and general outcomes

Any testimonial, review, illustration, or statement about a past experience is individual to the person who provided it and is not a promise that another person will have the same experience or outcome. Where a testimonial is published by Anima Space, the practice should obtain and retain the appropriate permission before publication and should avoid presenting a testimonial in a way that reveals a client’s identity or clinical information without valid consent.
