## 2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Anima Space website and its contact, enquiry, booking, telephone, email, WhatsApp, and online-session communications. It covers the information collected through the website’s publicly visible contact form and booking flow, including information about children and adolescents supplied by a parent or lawful guardian.

This policy does not replace a separate informed-consent document, client agreement, clinical-record notice, tele-counselling consent, child and adolescent consent arrangement, or other notice that Anima Space may provide before professional services begin. If there is a conflict, the more specific client or service notice will apply to the relevant professional record to the extent permitted by law.

Third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Meet, Google Maps, email systems, telephone networks, hosting providers, calendar tools, and any future payment or scheduling provider have their own privacy notices. This policy does not control their independent processing.

We collect information that a person voluntarily provides and information that may be generated when the website or communications are used. The categories below reflect the current public site and should be checked against the live implementation before this policy is published.

The website is not an emergency service and is not monitored continuously.

The booking flow currently displayed on the website does not show a payment-card or payment-processor step. If Anima Space later accepts online payments, the practice must update this policy before collecting payment data and identify the payment provider and its role.

## 4. How we use personal data

Anima Space may process personal data for the following purposes:

1. To receive and respond to contact, service, career-counselling, training, or general enquiries.
2. To assess at a preliminary level whether the requested service or practitioner appears appropriate and, where necessary, suggest a different service or referral.
3. To schedule, confirm, reschedule, cancel, and administer appointments, including online video-call links and in-clinic location details.
4. To communicate through the contact method selected or supplied by the person, including telephone, email, WhatsApp, or other agreed channels.
5. To provide counselling, coaching, career counselling, well-being support, parent guidance, training, or other services that the person requests and accepts.
6. To maintain appropriate professional, safeguarding, operational, accounting, legal, complaint, and insurance records.
7. To protect the website, systems, clients, practitioners, and the public against fraud, misuse, unauthorised access, or security incidents.
8. To comply with applicable legal obligations, lawful requests, court orders, professional requirements, and safeguarding duties.
9. To understand website performance and improve content or services, but only using analytics, cookies, or similar tools that Anima Space has actually enabled and disclosed.
10. To send service-related communications. Marketing or promotional communications will be sent only where a lawful basis and any required consent exist, and each message will provide a practical way to stop receiving such communications.

Anima Space will seek to limit collection and use to information reasonably necessary for the stated purpose. India’s DPDP Act requires a notice describing the personal data and purpose of processing, and it states that consent should be free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous, and limited to data necessary for the specified purpose.[5] The practice should present the website notice in clear language and obtain any consent required for the actual processing activity.

## 5. Children and adolescent information

Anima Space advertises services for children and adolescents, including services described for ages 8–18. A parent or lawful guardian should complete the website contact or booking form for a person under 18 and provide any consent or authority required for the requested service. Anima Space may request reasonable information to confirm the parent’s or guardian’s authority and may pause or decline a request if required consent or safeguarding information is unavailable.

Anima Space will not knowingly use a child’s personal data for targeted advertising, behavioural profiling, or any other activity prohibited by applicable law. The practice should confirm its child-data verification and consent process, including how it handles mature adolescents, separated parents, guardians, school referrals, and safeguarding situations, before publishing this policy.

The amount and timing of information shared with a parent or guardian about a child’s sessions will be explained in the informed-consent and service documents. Confidentiality for children and adolescents may be subject to age, maturity, safety, safeguarding, professional, and legal considerations. This website policy does not by itself decide those clinical arrangements.

## 6. Confidentiality is separate from website privacy

Anima Space aims to keep professional information confidential, but confidentiality is not absolute. Information may be used or disclosed with appropriate consent, when required or permitted by law, where necessary to address a serious or imminent safety risk, where safeguarding or suspected abuse concerns arise, under a lawful court or authority request, for professional consultation or referral where permitted, or where otherwise necessary to provide the requested service.

The exact exceptions, record-access arrangements, parent or guardian involvement, and communication rules will be explained before professional services begin. A website contact form, WhatsApp chat, or email enquiry should not be treated as a secure clinical record or as a promise that every message will remain confidential from every third party involved in delivering the communication service.

## 7. Sharing and disclosure

Anima Space does not intend to sell or rent personal data. The practice may share the minimum information reasonably necessary with the following categories of recipients:

| Recipient category | Examples of sharing |
| --- | --- |
| Service providers and processors | Website hosting, form handling, email, calendar or booking, cloud storage, technical support, security, and communications providers acting on Anima Space’s instructions |
| Communication and meeting providers | WhatsApp, telephone providers, email services, Google Meet, or another platform selected for an appointment or enquiry |
| Professional or operational advisers | Accountants, legal advisers, insurers, auditors, or practitioners involved in an appropriate referral or professional consultation, subject to applicable confidentiality and legal requirements |
| Authorities and safety recipients | A court, regulator, law-enforcement body, child-protection or safeguarding authority, emergency service, or other recipient where disclosure is required or permitted by law or reasonably necessary to protect a person from serious harm |
| Business-transfer recipients | A successor, purchaser, or professional adviser involved in a merger, restructuring, transfer, or closure, subject to lawful protections and notice requirements |

Anima Space should maintain written agreements or other appropriate controls with relevant processors and should publish or make available their identities or categories where required. A specific provider must be added to this policy if the website uses analytics, advertising, CRM, payment, scheduling, form, email-marketing, or clinical-record systems beyond the services identified above.

## 8. International processing and third-party services

Some third-party providers used for hosting, email, messaging, online meetings, maps, calendars, security, or storage may process information outside India. When this occurs, Anima Space will use the safeguards and comply with the transfer restrictions required by applicable law. Visitors should review the relevant provider’s privacy notice before using an external service.

Google Meet and Google Maps are Google services; WhatsApp is a Meta service. Clicking a third-party link, sending a message through a third-party service, joining a Google Meet call, or using a map may make information available to that provider under its own terms and privacy practices. Anima Space cannot guarantee the policies, security, availability, or processing decisions of an independent third party.

## 9. Cookies and technical information

The website may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies required for basic operation, security, preferences, or booking functionality. If Anima Space enables analytics, advertising, social-media pixels, session recording, or other non-essential technologies, the practice should list each tool, purpose, provider, retention, and consent mechanism here before activating it.

A browser may allow a visitor to block or delete cookies. Blocking necessary cookies may affect website or booking functionality. Anima Space should not state that it uses analytics or advertising cookies unless those tools are actually present in the deployed site.

## 10. Security

Anima Space will take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks of processing. These measures may include access controls, limited staff access, secure account practices, device protection, provider due diligence, backups where appropriate, and procedures for responding to suspected incidents.

No website, email, messaging service, device, network, or online meeting platform can be guaranteed completely secure. Visitors should use private devices and networks where possible, protect their accounts, avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information, and promptly notify Anima Space if they believe a communication or account has been misdirected or compromised.

India’s DPDP Act places responsibility on the Data Fiduciary for appropriate technical and organisational measures, reasonable security safeguards, and applicable breach intimation requirements.[5] The practice should confirm its incident-response process and the current legal notification procedure before publication.

## 11. Retention and deletion

Anima Space will retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide or document requested services, to resolve complaints, to protect legal interests, to comply with professional, accounting, tax, insurance, safeguarding, or other legal obligations, and to establish or defend claims. When information is no longer required, Anima Space will delete it, anonymise it, or securely dispose of it, subject to lawful retention requirements and backup cycles.

The practice should insert its actual retention schedule before publication. The following table is a planning framework, not a statement that these periods have already been adopted:

| Record type | Proposed retention statement to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Unconverted website enquiry | Retain only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, close, and document the enquiry; then delete or anonymise unless a lawful reason requires longer retention |
| Appointment and communication records | Retain for the period reasonably necessary to administer the appointment, handle follow-up, complaints, accounting, insurance, and legal requirements |
| Professional or clinical records | Retain according to the practice’s informed-consent documents, applicable professional standards, and legal requirements; do not use the website policy as a substitute for the clinical-record schedule |
| Marketing permissions and opt-outs | Retain as needed to honour the person’s communication choices and demonstrate compliance |
| Security, access, and technical logs | Retain only for the period reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, and legal obligations |

India’s DPDP Act provides for erasure when consent is withdrawn or when the specified purpose is no longer served, unless retention is necessary under law.[5] A deletion request may therefore be limited where Anima Space must retain information for a lawful, professional, safeguarding, accounting, dispute, or security purpose.

## 12. Your choices and privacy requests

Subject to applicable law and reasonable verification of identity and authority, a person may contact Anima Space to ask what personal data is being processed, request access to relevant information, request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information, request erasure where retention is no longer necessary, withdraw consent where consent is the basis of processing, ask about the purposes and categories of sharing, or raise a complaint about the handling of personal data. India’s DPDP Act identifies rights relating to access, correction and erasure, grievance redressal, and nomination, subject to the Act and applicable rules.[5]

A request should be sent to **animaspace9@gmail.com** with the subject **“Privacy Request — Anima Space”**. The requester should identify the relevant email address or phone number, describe the request, and avoid sending unnecessary clinical information in the first message. Anima Space may request reasonable information to confirm identity, parent or guardian authority, and the scope of the request. The practice will respond within the period required by applicable law or, where no period applies, within a reasonable time.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal. It may also prevent Anima Space from responding to an enquiry, administering an appointment, or continuing a requested service when the information is necessary for that purpose. A person may contact Anima Space first to seek resolution of a privacy concern and may use any external grievance or regulatory mechanism available under applicable law.

## 13. Marketing communications

Anima Space may send service-related messages needed to answer an enquiry, confirm or change an appointment, provide a meeting link, or communicate an operational matter. Promotional or newsletter messages will be sent only where permitted by law and, where required, after a separate affirmative choice. Each promotional email should include an unsubscribe method, and WhatsApp or telephone marketing should stop when a person asks not to receive it, subject to messages strictly necessary to administer an existing service.

The current website content does not show a separate marketing opt-in. Anima Space should add one before collecting marketing consent and should not treat a request for counselling or an appointment as consent to unrelated marketing.

## 14. Third-party links

A link does not mean that Anima Space controls or endorses that site’s privacy practices. Visitors should read the relevant third-party privacy notice before submitting information or joining a third-party service.
